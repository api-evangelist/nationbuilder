Clear, well-organized communication can make or break your team’s success. Whether you're managing regional offices, volunteers, or multiple departments, ensuring the right people have the right tools is essential. That’s why we’re excited to extend Groups functionality to broadcasters—allowing you to control who can access and send messages from specific broadcasters based on their Group.
